## Release workflow (new)

`.github/workflows/release-bin.yml` builds the firmware on a GitHub runner and
attaches `AlthermaInterface-<version>.bin` and a `.sha256` to the release.
It runs on a `v*` tag push, or by hand for an existing tag (`workflow_dispatch`
with `tag`). It refuses to publish when the tag does not match
`FIRMWARE_VERSION` in `main/version.h`.

- Test run: `v1.18.0` attached by hand, 1,175,664 bytes. Passed.
- Real run: `v1.20.0` attached by the tag push, 1,181,136 bytes. Passed; the
  checksum verifies. Pages manifest also reports 1.20.0.

The token needs the `workflow` scope to push changes under
`.github/workflows/`. It was added during this session.

## External MQTT sensors (1.20.0)

The device subscribes to a read-only list of MQTT topics and shows their
latest values on the Home page, in shaded dashed boxes with their age.

- Config tab: one entry per line as `label | topic | unit`, up to 16 entries.
  The list is checked as a whole; a malformed list gets a 400 and nothing is
  saved. Topics must be exact, with no `+` or `#`.
- Stored in NVS as `ext_topics`, `;` between entries, up to 1,024 bytes.
- Code: `main/external.c`, `main/external.h`; subscribe on connect and
  `alt_mqtt_subscribe()` in `main/mqtt.c`; `GET /api/external`.
- The rule in FSD §2 changed from "subscribes to no topic" to "subscribes only
  to read-only external topics". `CLAUDE.md` and `README.md` say the same.

## Curve setpoint: what is known

- Register `0x54` offset 12 (`Delta-Tr`) was a constant 33 until about 20 Sep,
  then moved between 30 and 42 on each later day. Source: 23 cycle logs in
  `captures/`, Sep 12 to Sep 25.
- The field-settings capture (12 Sep) had the curve anchors Lo_A −20 °C,
  Ti 40 °C; Hi_A 10 °C, Ti 25 °C. At 8 °C outdoor the calculated target was
  26 °C, but the unit shows 33 in curve mode. So the anchors have changed since
  12 Sep, or the local shift (D-03) is set.
- Display check on the unit at 8 °C outdoor: a short press of the
  weather-dependent button shows 33. Outlet water was 36.5 °C.
- Working assumption: `Delta-Tr` is the weather-dependent leaving-water target.
  Published as "Curve setpoint (C)", a temperature sensor, from 1.17.0. HA
  history began 1 Nov 10:04, so the earlier data is not in HA.
- Not verified: the anchors and shift now in use, and whether the register
  follows outdoor air across a day. The outdoor air topic was not yet logging
  when this was written.

R-C Setpoint (`0x54` offset 13) read 0 in every capture, and is still 0.

## Lessons and traps (recorded so they are not repeated)

- **Config POST without `user` clears the MQTT login.** The handler treats an
  absent field as empty for `user`. It happened today. Always send `uri` and
  `user` with any POST to `/api/config`. The password is kept when `pass` is
  omitted.
- **Config POST reboots the device.** The response is `Saved. Rebooting...`.
  Expect a short gap.
- **JSON from Python needs `ensure_ascii=False` and `separators=(',', ':')`.**
  The device's `json_field()` does not decode `\u` escapes and needs no space
  after the colon. The first attempts were refused for both reasons.
- **Script strings in `web_server.c`:** a `\n` in a C string reaches the
  browser as a real newline, which breaks JavaScript. Write `\\n` for the JS
  escape. This caused "Invalid or unexpected token" in 1.20.0's first build.
- **Rebuild before OTA when the version changed**, or the old image goes up
  (1.15.0 lesson, still true).
- **Firmware `.bin` files are not in git** (`.gitignore` excludes `*.bin`).
  They come from the Pages workflow and, for releases, from the release
  workflow.
- **Python on this machine:** the IDF environment is at
  `D:\esp\tools\python_env\idf6.0_py3.11_env`. The `C:\Users\Stein` path in
  CLAUDE.md does not exist on this machine.
- **The HA token** is read from the `HA_Token` environment variable only. It is
  never written to a file or printed. HA is at `http://192.168.10.151:8123`
  (REST and WebSocket). The WebSocket `mqtt/subscribe` command with topic `#`
  listens on the broker for a short while; it is how the external topics were
  found.
